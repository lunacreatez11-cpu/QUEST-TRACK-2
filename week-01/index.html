How to play

  1. Press Start. The box turns red — wait!
  2. When it turns green and says GO!, click the box as fast as you can.
  3. Clicking while it's still red = Too early! (counts as a fail).
  4. Try again to beat your best time. Tip: you can also press the Space key.
